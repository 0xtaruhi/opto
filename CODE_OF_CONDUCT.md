# Code of conduct

Opto contributors and maintainers are expected to make participation a
harassment-free experience, use welcoming and inclusive language, accept
constructive feedback, and focus disagreement on technical decisions rather
than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discriminatory language, personal
attacks, deliberate intimidation, publishing another person's private
information, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer contact in
`CITATION.cff`. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior violates this
policy. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces. It is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
